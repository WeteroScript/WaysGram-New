# Channel views and reach

Android requests view counts for loaded channel post cells. The server counts at most one view per account per post, excluding the channel owner, and only accepts reports from an account with current access to that channel. Client reports are an engagement estimate, not proof a human read a post. Reports on deleted or foreign posts do not count.

The owner analytics dialog reports unique account–post pairs and distinct accounts across those pairs for the selected 7-, 30-, or 90-day window. Each pair is attributed to its latest reported view. Deleting a post also removes its view data. Historical views before this feature launched are not backfilled.
